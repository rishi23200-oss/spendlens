# Unit Economics

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assume:
- Average deal: $3,000/yr in AI credits purchased
- Gross margin on credits: ~25% (sourced at discount, sold below retail)
- Gross profit per customer: ~$750/yr
- Average customer lifetime: 2 years
- LTV: ~$1,500

## CAC by Channel

| Channel | Est. CAC | Basis |
|---------|----------|-------|
| HN Show HN post | ~$0 | Time only; 200–500 visits if it hits front page |
| Reddit organic | ~$0 | Time only |
| Newsletter mention | ~$200 | Estimated cost of sponsored mention if paid |
| X/Twitter DMs | ~$0 | Time only |
| Referral from audit share | ~$0 | Viral loop built into product |

Blended CAC assumption: **$50** (accounting for founder time at $100/hr, ~30 min per 
acquired customer across all channels)

## Conversion Funnel

| Stage | Rate | Basis |
|-------|------|-------|
| Landing → Audit started | 40% | Strong value prop, no friction |
| Audit started → Completed | 70% | Form is short, state persists |
| Completed → Email captured | 25% | Shown after value delivered |
| Email captured → Consultation booked | 10% | High-savings cases surfaced prominently |
| Consultation → Credit purchase | 30% | Warm lead, real savings identified |

**End-to-end: 100 visitors → 40 audits → 10 emails → 1 consultation → 0.3 purchases**

At $750 gross profit and $50 CAC: **LTV:CAC = 30:1**. Highly profitable if conversion holds.

## $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~333 credit customers at $3,000/yr average

**What has to be true:**
- 33,000 audit completions over 18 months (~60/day by month 6)
- 8,250 email captures (25% of completions)
- 825 consultations booked (10% of emails)
- 333 purchases (40% of consultations — higher than avg because audits pre-qualify)

**Traffic required:** ~110,000 unique visitors over 18 months (~200/day)

This is achievable with: 1 viral HN post (5,000 visits), consistent Reddit presence 
(2,000/mo), newsletter mentions (1,000 each), and compounding referral traffic from 
shared audit URLs. Not guaranteed, but not fantasy math either.

## Sensitivity
The model breaks if:
- Consultation → purchase rate drops below 15% (tool attracts wrong ICP)
- Average deal size drops below $1,500 (small teams, small spend)
- Audit completion rate drops below 50% (form too long)

These are the three numbers to instrument first.

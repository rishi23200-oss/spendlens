# Go-To-Market Plan

## Exact Target User
**Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5–25 person seed/Series A startup**, paying $500–5,000/mo 
across 3–6 AI tools, who has never done a formal audit. They approved the tools ad-hoc as 
engineers requested them and now the bill is opaque. Their job title on LinkedIn: 
"Head of Engineering", "CTO", "VP Engineering". Company stage: post-product, pre-scale.

## What They Google/Scroll Before Wanting This
- "cursor vs github copilot 2026"
- "is claude team worth it for small team"
- "ai tools for dev team budget"
- Scrolling a Twitter/X thread comparing AI coding tools
- Reading a HN comment thread about AI infrastructure costs

## Where They Hang Out Online
- **Hacker News** — /new and comment threads on AI tool posts
- **r/ExperiencedDevs**, **r/cscareerquestions**, **r/startups**
- **Lenny's Slack** (product/eng leaders)
- **Rands Leadership Slack**
- **X/Twitter** — following @swyx, @levelsio, @paul_copplestone
- **IndieHackers** — founders tracking costs carefully

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

**Week 1 — Seeding**
- Post on HN "Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor for dev teams"
- DM 20 CTOs on X who have recently tweeted about AI tool costs
- Post in Lenny's Slack #tools channel with a genuine question framing

**Week 2 — Communities**
- Post in r/ExperiencedDevs: "I audited our $800/mo AI stack and found $300 in waste — here's what I found and a tool I built"
- Cross-post to r/startups and IndieHackers
- Submit to daily.dev and Hashnode

**Week 3 — Distribution partnerships**
- Reach out to 5 engineering newsletter writers (TLDR, Pointer, Engineering Weekly) for a mention
- Ask 3 YC founders in network to share with their batch

**Week 4 — Compounding**
- Shareable audit URLs drive organic word of mouth (each shared audit = implicit endorsement)
- Follow up with HN/Reddit commenters who engaged

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex has direct relationships with AI vendors. A co-marketing post from Anthropic or 
Cursor's official channels ("here's a free tool to right-size your plan") reaches exactly 
the right audience with implied endorsement. No competitor has this.

## Week-1 Traction if This Works
- 300+ audit completions
- 80+ email captures (>25% conversion)
- 15+ shares of the shareable URL
- 3+ organic mentions in Slack communities we didn't post in

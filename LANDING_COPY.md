## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial, no account required. You get the full audit on-screen immediately.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every number is pulled from official vendor pricing pages and updated weekly. Sources are cited in our public PRICING_DATA.md.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your tool and spend data is used only to generate your audit. We store it temporarily to power your shareable link. If you provide your email, we store that to send your report.

**Q: What if I'm already spending optimally?**
A: We'll tell you that. We don't manufacture savings. If your stack is well-configured, the audit says so — and offers to notify you when better options appear.

**Q: How is this related to Credex?**
A: SpendLens is a free tool built by Credex. Credex sells discounted AI credits to startups. For audits showing significant savings, we'll show you how Credex can help capture more of that savings.
